# Analysis Notes and Interpretation Guide

This file is a guide to interpreting query outputs. Because the records are synthetic and randomly generated, do not present any numeric results as market benchmarks.

## 1. Executive scorecard
Start with pipeline size, won ACV, closed-deal count, win rate, and average won ACV. Check whether the dashboard uses consistent date filters and definitions.

## 2. Regional and sales-motion performance
Compare volume and outcomes together. A high win rate on a small sample may be less reliable than a moderate win rate on a large sample. Investigate differences in deal size, segment mix, and sales-cycle length before recommending a territory or channel change.

## 3. Discount analysis
Treat discount bands as exploratory. Discounting may be higher on more competitive or strategically important deals, so the analysis cannot establish causality. Include closed-deal counts beside the rates.

## 4. Stale pipeline
Use the stale-opportunity query as a review queue, not an automated judgment about rep performance. Agree on a business definition of a stale opportunity, and consider stage, expected close date, next step, and deal context.

## 5. Lost reasons
Use loss reasons to frame follow-up questions. Check whether reps apply loss reasons consistently and whether “No decision” or “Timing” should be separated from competitive losses.

## Suggested executive narrative template

> **What changed?** [Describe a supported trend with time period and metric.]
>
> **Why might it matter?** [Explain potential impact on pipeline quality, bookings, or GTM efficiency.]
>
> **What should we investigate next?** [Propose a test or additional slice of data.]
>
> **Recommended action:** [Small, measurable action with an owner and review date.]

Avoid making causal claims unless the analysis design supports them.
